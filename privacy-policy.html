Privacy Policy

Holy Moments: Prayer Reminders

Last updated: November 13, 2025

Overview

This privacy policy explains how Holy Moments ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application on iOS or Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from you.

Our app operates entirely on your device and does not transmit, store, or collect any personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Device information
  • Any other personal information

Platform Analytics and Store Data

While our app itself does not collect any data, Apple and Google may automatically collect certain information through their respective app store platforms. This may include:

  • App download and installation data
  • Store page views and interactions
  • Basic app performance metrics
  • Crash reports (if you have opted in to share analytics with your platform provider)

This information is collected by Apple or Google, not by us, and is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can control your data and analytics sharing preferences in your device's system settings under the appropriate privacy sections.

Data Storage and Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal information for us to store or secure. All app functionality operates locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with any third-party services that would collect your personal information.

Subscriptions and Purchases

If you choose to subscribe to premium features, payments are securely processed by Apple (for iOS users) or Google (for Android users). We do not have access to your payment information. All billing and subscription management are handled by the respective platform you used to subscribe.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no additional privacy considerations for children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data for you to access, modify, or delete. If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us using the information below.
